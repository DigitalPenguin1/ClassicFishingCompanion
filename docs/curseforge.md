# Summary

Fishing addon for WoW Classic Era, TBC and Forever. Double right-click to cast with auto-lure, a stats HUD, one-click gear swaps, catch tracking by zone, goals, and a bobber splash you can actually hear.

# Description

**Everything you need while fishing in WoW Classic Era, TBC and WoW: Forever.** Double right-click to cast and your lure goes on by itself. A small HUD tracks your catches, fish per hour, skill and lure timer. Every fish you catch is logged by zone, with no setup.

> **One download, three games.** The same zip runs on Classic Era, TBC Anniversary and WoW: Forever. Forever gets its own build with side tabs, cast bar timers and the game's Equipment Manager for gear sets.

## Casting

- **Easy Cast**: double right-click anywhere to cast. If your lure has run out, it's put back on first. A single right-click on the bobber still loots normally
- **Lure Manager**: pick a lure on the Lure tab and the addon keeps it applied. You get a chat note if you run out
- **Captain Rumsey's Lager (TBC)**: drinks it before casting when the buff is gone
- **Auto-swap combat weapons**: get attacked with a pole in hand and you swap to your weapons, then back to the pole when combat ends

## Stats HUD

- Session and total catches, fish per hour, and your skill with pole and lure bonuses
- **Lure timer** with time left. On Forever it's the game's cast bar, turning yellow under 2 minutes and red in the last one
- **Apply Lure** and **Swap** buttons right on the HUD
- Up to 3 **Goals** with progress
- Default, Minimal or Text Only look, drag it anywhere, and scale it from 75% to 150%

## Tracking

- **Catch List**: every fish and item you've pulled up, with counts, share of your catches, and the best hour to catch it
- **Zones**: everything you've caught, grouped by zone, with unique species and each zone's share
- **Statistics**: hourly, daily and weekly bars, top catches, and zone productivity
- **Goals**: set a catch target for a fish. Up to 3 show on the HUD and they reset each session
- **Catch & Release**: mark fish you don't want and delete them from your bags with one key. They still count in your stats

## Gear sets

Save your fishing gear once and swap to it from the HUD, the minimap button, a keybinding or `/cfc swap`. Whatever you were wearing is saved first, so swapping back works with any spec. On Forever the sets live in the game's Equipment Manager, so they're stored on the server.

## Alerts and sound

- **Enhance Bobber Splash**: while you fish, the game's sound turns on even if you muted it, and music, ambience, dialog, pet and emote sounds go quiet, so the splash stands out. A Splash Volume slider sets how loud. Your sound settings come back 10 seconds after you stop
- **Rare fish** sound when you land one of the rare catches
- **Milestones** for catch counts, with optional announcements to guild, party or say
- **No Fishing Lure!** warning when you cast without a lure

## Your data

- **Automatic backups** every 24 hours, with one-click restore from Settings
- **Per-character mode** if you'd rather keep stats per character, with a copy of your account data to start from
- **Quiet Mode** cuts chat messages down to errors and warnings

## Getting around

- **Minimap button**: left-click for the window, right-click for the HUD, hover for a quick stats summary
- **Keybindings** for the window, the HUD and Release Fish under ESC > Options > Keybindings
- `/cfc` opens the window. `/cfc hud`, `/cfc swap` and `/cfc stats` work too, and the [wiki](https://github.com/DigitalPenguin1/ClassicFishingCompanion/wiki) lists the rest
